PinyinMe dictionary data
========================

This directory contains a derivative of CC-CEDICT.

CC-CEDICT is published by MDBG and licensed under the
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License.

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cc-cedict

Referenced work: CEDICT, copyright 1997, 1998 Paul Andrew Denisowski.

The release used here is dated 2026-11-12 (125,408 entries).
PinyinMe repacked the entries and added HSK levels and frequency ranks.
That derivative stays under CC BY-SA 4.0. You may copy, share and adapt
it under the same license, with attribution.

HSK levels and frequency ranks are joined from
drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary (MIT License),
commit 7ac65bf1a6387d35f1ade478906172a19311c7f9.

- HSK 2.0 uses that project's old-* levels (the previous six-level lists).
- HSK 3.0 uses that project's newest-* levels, the 2026 syllabus.
  Band 7 covers levels 7–9.
- Frequency ranks are the ranks published in that compilation.
  They trace to the BCC/BLCU corpus list circulated with Pleco.

Changes: entries were merged on simplified form, traditional form and
pinyin; classifier notes were split out; HSK and frequency fields were
added; the file was compressed.

The PinyinMe application code is separate from this data.
